Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Greenville

A little routine care is the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a few miserable nights.
